Discover the main events happening around campus.

Sports

Sports activities bring energy, teamwork, and healthy competition to our campus event. Students can participate in various indoor and outdoor games, showcase their talents, build friendships, and enjoy an exciting experience together.

Freshers' Party

The Freshers' Party is a vibrant campus event welcoming new students, taking place on 5 September. It includes music, dance, games, introductions, and fun activities, helping freshers make friends and create memorable moments together.

Dance & Music

Dance is a vibrant campus event where students express creativity, energy, and emotions through different dance styles. Participants perform individually or in groups, showcasing talent, rhythm, confidence, and enthusiasm.

Music is an exciting campus event where students showcase their vocal and instrumental talents. Participants perform various genres, entertain the audience, and create a lively atmosphere filled with creativity, rhythm, and passion.

Hackathon

A Hackathon is an exciting campus event where students collaborate, innovate, and develop creative solutions to real-world problems using technology. Participants showcase their skills, teamwork, and ideas while competing for exciting prizes.
